Section-by-Section Page Specifications
1. Home Page/
1
Hero
Full-viewport dark background. Logo top-left. Brand statement center: "Curated in Kathmandu. From the world. For the world." Tagline below. Two large CTA buttons: NATURE (→/nature) and ENERGY (→/energy). Background: cinematic still or ambient loop (Boudha/mountain). Gold divider line.
2
Brand Strip
3-column bar: "Kathmandu, Nepal — Our Lens, Not Our Limit / Sourced Globally. Served Globally. / Rooted in Respect. Guided by Energy." Thin gold dividers. Cream background strip.
3
Shaman Stories — Latest
Full-width featured video (latest Reel/story). Section header: "Shaman Stories". Autoplay muted loop. Play button overlay. "View All Stories" link below.
4
New Releases
Horizontal scroll or 4-col grid. Each card: product image, name, element badge, energy tag, price, Add to Cart. Filter tabs above: All / Metal / Earth / Wood / Plant / Water / Air.
5
Shaman Member Strip
Dark gold-accent band. "Become a Shaman Member." Benefits in 3 icons. CTA: Join Now (→ register or scrolls to signup form). Threshold: NPR 1,500+ purchase.
6
Energy Services Preview
2–3 service cards: Sound Healing, Sun Yoga, Breath Yoga. Element color accents. Brief description. Book via WhatsApp CTA per card. "Explore All Services" link.
7
All Categories Grid
6 element cards (preview, not full listing). Each: element icon, name, 2-line energy description. Click → /nature/[element]. Dark background, hover lifts card with gold border.
8
Footer
Logo. Nav links. 4 location addresses. Social icons (Instagram primary). WhatsApp button. Privacy / Terms. "Nature does not carry a passport. Neither do we." closing line.
2. All Categories — Nature + Energy/nature
1
Banner
Full-width dark banner. "Nature + Energy" headline. Tagline: "Everything carries energy. Discover yours." Filter tabs: All / Metal / Earth / Wood / Plant / Water / Air.
2
Category Cards — Hover Reveal
6 large element cards in 3×2 grid. Default state: element name + icon + nature source. Hover state: slides up to reveal energy description + "Explore [Element]" CTA. Card gets element's accent color border.
3
Footer
Standard footer.
3. Individual Product Page/products/[slug]
1
Breadcrumb
Home → Nature → [Element] → [Product Name]. Gold text. Small, clean.
2
Product Gallery
Left: large image + thumbnail strip. Click to zoom. Right: Product name, element badge, energy badge, price. Quantity selector. Add to Cart (gold). WhatsApp Enquire (outline).
3
Description Tabs
3 tabs: About / How to Use / Element Story. About: product description. How to Use: care/usage instructions. Element Story: the nature source narrative (brand storytelling).
4
Related Products
4 products from same element. Horizontal scroll on mobile.
4. Shaman Stories/stories
1
Banner + Featured Story
Latest story as hero video embed. Full-width, autoplay muted. "Shaman Stories" label in Cormorant Garamond.
2
Category Filter
Filter by element or service type. Pill-style tabs. All / Metal / Earth / Wood / Plant / Water / Air / Sound Healing / Yoga.
3
Stories Grid
Masonry or 3-col grid. Each card: thumbnail, story title, element tag, date. Click → /stories/[slug] for individual story page with video + related products.
5. Checkout Flow/checkout
1
Step 1 — Cart Review
Item list, qty edit, remove. Order total. Member discount shown if applicable. "Proceed to Delivery" CTA.
2
Step 2 — Delivery Info
Name, phone, address, delivery notes. Kathmandu zone selector (Thamel / Jhamsikhel / Gongabu or shipping). Order summary sidebar.
3
Step 3 — Payment
Nepal payment options: eSewa, Khalti, Cash on Delivery, Bank Transfer. Backend handles gateway integration.
4
Confirmation
Order number + summary. If purchase ≥ NPR 1,500 → Member enrollment prompt. WhatsApp confirmation option. "Continue Shopping" CTA.

Gaps & Strategic Flags
Critical — Must resolve before launch
Payment Gateway: Nepal-specific — eSewa, Khalti, COD must be confirmed with backend dev before checkout UI is built. Which gateways are integrated?
Delivery / Shipping Logic: Are you delivering only in Kathmandu valley or Nepal-wide? Shipping cost calculation logic needs to be defined before step 2 of checkout.
WhatsApp number per location: Thamel, Jhamsikhel, Gongabu — confirm which numbers route to which store for service bookings.
Member Threshold Logic: NPR 1,500+ purchase triggers member status. Is this tracked in the backend? Needs API endpoint for frontend to display member badge and discounts.
Important — Affects UX significantly
Nepali Language Toggle: Your brand voice is Nepali-English mixed. Will the website be bilingual (NE/EN toggle) or English-primary with Nepali phrases woven in? This affects copy writing scope.
Product data structure: Each product needs: name, images (multiple), element, energy type, category, price, description, usage, story. Confirm these fields exist in your backend schema before frontend builds product detail.
Stories → Product link: Individual story pages should link to products featured in that story. Needs a "featured_products" field on the story content model in the backend.
Online-only vs. showroom products: Some products may be showroom-only (singing bowl sound healing). How does the UI differentiate? "Available Online" vs. "Visit Showroom" states needed.
Canned Oxygen on website: Priority product but has specific storage/delivery logistics. May need an "enquire before purchase" flow rather than direct add-to-cart.
Strategic — Will affect growth post-launch
Instagram/TikTok embed on Stories page: Shaman Stories are Instagram Reels first. Embed directly from Instagram API or use a social feed widget — saves duplicating video hosting.
SEO from day one: URL structure should be /nature/metal/singing-bowl not /products/12345. Human-readable slugs by element → huge SEO advantage for "singing bowl Kathmandu" searches.
WhatsApp pre-filled message: Every "Enquire on WhatsApp" button should pre-fill: "Hi, I'm interested in [product name] — [URL]". Dramatically increases conversion from enquiry to sale.
Open Graph tags for sharing: Product pages shared on WhatsApp/Instagram should show rich previews (image, name, price). Needs OG meta tags on product detail template.
